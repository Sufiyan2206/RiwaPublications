Nursery, Pre Primary 1 and Pre Primary 2

Early years curriculum

Learning through play

Young children learn best by doing, exploring and talking about what they see. Our early years books make that first experience of school joyful and gentle, and build confidence one small step at a time.

See early years books

How we teach

  • Play and activity. Children learn through doing, not only through listening.
  • Step-by-step progression. Each class builds gently on the one before.
  • Links to daily life. Ideas start from the home, family and world children know.
  • Connected learning. Related ideas are taught together, so learning makes sense as a whole.
  • Practice that stays. Each Workbook gives children time to try and repeat new skills.

Five areas of growth

Thinking

Noticing, sorting, matching and solving simple problems.

Language

Listening, speaking and early reading and writing.

Physical

Hand control and movement through hands-on tasks.

Social

Sharing, taking turns and working with others.

Emotional

Understanding feelings and growing in confidence.

Class 1 to Class 5

Primary curriculum

Growing curious about the world

As children grow, they become active learners. The child is at the centre, and the teacher guides. Our primary books help children ask questions, explore ideas and connect what they learn to the world around them.

See primary books

How we teach

  • Learning through themes. Topics are built around things children can relate to.
  • Connected subjects. Children see how ideas link across different subjects.
  • Many ways to learn. Stories, pictures, activities and discussion suit different learners.
  • Learning by asking. Questions encourage children to think, not only to remember.
  • Steady progress. Concepts deepen from one class to the next, from Class 1 to Class 5.
  • Practice that stays. Each Workbook helps children apply and review what they learned.
